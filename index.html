Know what your sport really costs

Add what you pay for club fees, coaching, gear, travel and the rest. SportCost turns it into a cost per year and per month, shows where the money goes, and lets you test a change before you make it.

Open the calculator See how it works

Three steps. No account.

  1. Enter what you pay

    Choose your sport and currency, then fill in each cost. Say how often you pay: once, weekly, monthly, per season or per event.

  2. See the real total

    Get the cost per year and per month. Gear you buy once is shown separately, so it is never counted twice.

  3. Compare a change

    Copy your budget, change one part such as gear or travel, and see what it does to the yearly cost.

Your budget

Changing the currency changes the symbol only. Amounts are not converted.

SportCost is an early version. It is built to be tested with real athletes and families, and to improve from what they tell us. Your numbers stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Example amounts are placeholders that show how the tool works. They are not averages and not research. Advice lists questions to ask. It does not promise any discount or programme.
